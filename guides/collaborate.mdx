Game design is a team sport. Magic Forge lets you invite writers, artists, engineers, and producers into the same workspace so feedback stays tied to the elements it references. You control who can edit, who can comment, and who can only view, all without leaving the project.

## Roles and permissions

Every project member has one of three roles. The table below shows what each role can do in the web app.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
    Full control over the project. Can edit content, manage members, change roles, publish and unpublish, delete the project, and access version history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Editor" icon="pencil">
    Can create, edit, and delete elements and design notes. Can add comments and suggestions. Cannot manage members, change roles, or delete the project.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
    Can view all project content, read comments, and export or copy read-only links. Cannot edit elements, add comments, or manage members.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Only Owners and Editors can leave comments on elements. Viewers see comments but cannot reply or create new threads.
</Note>

## Invite teammates

You can invite collaborators by email or by generating a shareable invite link.

### Invite by email

1. Open the project and click **Share**.
2. Enter the teammate's email address.
3. Select a role: Owner, Editor, or Viewer.
4. Click **Send Invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a link to join. If they do not have a Magic Forge account, they will be prompted to create one.

### Invite by link

1. In the **Share** panel, toggle **Invite Link** to on.
2. Choose the default role for link joins (usually Editor or Viewer).
3. Copy the link and send it through your team chat or email.

You can revoke the link at any time to stop new joins.

## Comments and suggestions

Open any element and click the **Comments** tab to start a discussion thread. Mention a teammate with `@name` to notify them. Comments are threaded, resolved, and preserved in version history, so you can trace why a value changed long after the conversation ends.

Editors and Owners can also leave **Suggestions**, which are proposed edits to element attributes or descriptions. The project Owner can accept or reject each suggestion from the **Review** panel.

## Real-time presence

When multiple teammates are viewing or editing the same project, Magic Forge shows live cursors and user avatars in the element list. You will see who is online and which element they have open, reducing collisions and redundant work.

<Warning>
  Real-time presence shows activity only within the same project. It does not show what other projects a teammate is working on.
</Warning>

## Limits

- Each project supports up to 50 members.
- Comments are limited to 5,000 characters per thread.
- The **Review** panel retains up to 100 open suggestions at a time. Older suggestions are archived, not deleted.
- Invite links expire after 30 days if unused.

## Managing permissions

Owners can change a member's role or remove them at any time from the **Members** list in the **Share** panel. Changes take effect immediately. Removed members lose all access, but their comments and resolved suggestions remain in the project history.

## Result

Your team can now work inside the same project with clear roles, threaded feedback, live presence, and controlled access. Design decisions stay documented where they belong: next to the elements they affect.
